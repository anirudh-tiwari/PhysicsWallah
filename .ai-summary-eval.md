## What's Working Well
- The trigger is placed after the duration/date details in a wrapping flex row, with `margin-left: auto` to align it to the right while preserving the metadata.
- The trigger is conditionally rendered only for Lectures and only when matching course/lesson summary data exists; Notes, DPP, and DPP PDF do not expose it.
- The two active courses have three relevant bullets per lecture, and the dialog labels the content a “Sample recap” that is not generated from a video or transcript.
- The native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` supports Escape and modal semantics; close and backdrop handlers are present, and `onClose` returns focus to the saved trigger.
- The dialog and metadata controls have narrow-screen styling and visible keyboard focus indicators.
